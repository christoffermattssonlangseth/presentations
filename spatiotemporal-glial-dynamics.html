Disease Background

Multiple Sclerosis

  • Autoimmune neurodegenerative disease — immune system attacks the CNS
  • Myelin sheath surrounding axonal projections is progressively broken down
  • Oligodendrocytes, the myelinating cells of the CNS, are the primary target
  • Underlying factors include both environmental and genetic components
  • Disease course: relapse-remitting or chronic progressive
